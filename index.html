About Me

I am an atmospheric scientist with a background in atmospheric chemistry and regional-to-hemispheric air quality modeling. My work integrates WRF–CMAQ simulations, process-based diagnostics, and ground, aircraft, and satellite observations to investigate the transport and chemical transformation of air pollutants, particularly those associated with wildfires and anthropogenic emissions.

Research Interests

01 / MODELING

Atmospheric Chemistry & Air Quality

Numerical simulation of atmospheric composition, pollutant chemistry, and secondary pollutant formation with WRF–CMAQ.

02 / TRANSPORT

Wildfire Smoke & Long-range Transport

Understanding the emissions, chemical evolution, and downwind air-quality impacts of wildfire plumes.

03 / ANALYSIS

Process Analysis & Model Evaluation

Applying process analysis and observational datasets to identify the mechanisms controlling pollutant distributions.
