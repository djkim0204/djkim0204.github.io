About Me

I am an atmospheric scientist specializing in atmospheric chemistry and air quality modeling. My research focuses on the transport and chemical transformation of air pollutants using numerical models such as WRF-CMAQ.

Research Interests

  • Atmospheric Chemistry and Air Quality
  • WRF-CMAQ Modeling
  • Wildfire Emissions and Smoke Transport
  • Long-range Transport of Air Pollutants
  • Process Analysis and Source Attribution
